What does MEP stand for?

MEP stands for Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing — the building services that make a space safe, comfortable and functional, including HVAC, power, lighting, water supply, drainage and fire-fighting systems.

Why choose a single MEP company for my project?

One contractor coordinates every trade, which reduces clashes between services, speeds up approvals and gives you one point of responsibility from design to handover.

Do you handle DEWA and Civil Defense approvals?

Yes. We prepare drawings and submissions and manage approvals with Dubai Municipality, DEWA, Dubai Civil Defense and free-zone authorities on your behalf.

How long does an MEP installation take?

Timelines depend on the size and type of project. A villa or office fit-out typically takes 3–8 weeks, while larger commercial buildings are scheduled in phases alongside the main construction program.

Do you offer MEP maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer annual maintenance contracts covering HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire-fighting systems, with preventive visits and emergency support.
