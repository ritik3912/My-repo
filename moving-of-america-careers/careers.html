Who we are

Built by Friends, Run Like Family

Moving of America started in 2007 when two friends, David Bloom and Sammy Sassoun, set out with a few trucks and a crew made up mostly of family and friends, taking on local moves across New Jersey.

We've grown a lot since then, but we're still family-oriented and still based in Bergen County, with our headquarters in Ridgefield, NJ.

Growth & opportunity

We Promote From Within

We strongly believe in internal promotion and rewarding excellence. If you put in the effort, your dedication and hard work will be recognized and valued.

We're committed to supporting your progression and advancement within the company, from your first day on the truck to leading a crew.
